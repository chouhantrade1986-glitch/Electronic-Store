Grade A: Near-mint condition, no visible scratches from 30cm, 90%+ battery health. Grade B: Flawless display with minor cosmetic scratches on body, 85%+ battery health. Grade C: Visible cosmetic signs of use/light dents, 100% functional screen, 80%+ battery health with the deepest price discounts.
