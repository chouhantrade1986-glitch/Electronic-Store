Frequently Asked Questions

When will my old device be picked up?

Your old device is picked up at the exact same time your new product is delivered at your doorstep.

What if my old device fails the doorstep inspection?

If the device condition does not match the assessment, you can pay the exchange discount difference via UPI/card or cancel the exchange without penalty.

Do I need to provide the original box and charger?

Original box and charger are optional, but including them may earn you an extra ElectroMart Exchange Bonus of up to ₹500.

Is my personal data safe during trade-in?

Yes, all exchanged devices undergo a certified multi-pass military-grade data wipe before refurbishment or responsible recycling.
