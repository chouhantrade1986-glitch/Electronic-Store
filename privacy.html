ElectroMart Legal & Data Rights

Privacy, Consent & Data Rights

Effective date: September 17, 2026. This page explains the controls available to customers and is subject to legal review before production publication.

Consent preferences

ElectroMart uses essential local storage for cart, account, language and checkout continuity. No third-party advertising tracker is enabled by this storefront.

Your data rights

You may request a copy of locally stored profile data or request deletion of eligible locally stored account data. Identity verification may be required before any account-level action.

Data retention & security

  • Cart, language and preference data remain on the customer device until cleared or removed.
  • Authentication and order records are retained only for account, fulfilment, fraud prevention, tax and legal obligations.
  • Production retention periods, processors and deletion schedules require documented legal and security approval.

Grievance officer & legal contact

ElectroMart Grievance Officer
Email: legal@electromart.in
Response target: within 48 hours for acknowledgement.

For unresolved consumer concerns, include your order reference, registered contact method and a clear description of the issue. This contact disclosure must be verified against the operating entity before production launch.

This privacy hub is an engineering control surface, not legal advice or a certification of compliance with Indian law. The operating entity, privacy notice, consent wording, retention schedule and grievance process must be reviewed by qualified Indian counsel before production use.
