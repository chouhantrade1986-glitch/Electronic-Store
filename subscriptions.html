  • Tier 1 pays 5% off every cycle. Tier 2 pays 10%. Tier 3 pays 15%. The percentage depends only on how many lines you keep running, never on the cadence you pick.
  • Forecasts below count six cycles from today. They are a projection at list prices, not a quotation, and your own basket decides the real figure.
