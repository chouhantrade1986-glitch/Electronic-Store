        Plate vi — the handover

        How EMI appears at checkout

        Choosing an instalment plan is a separate step from paying. Here is exactly what happens, and exactly what you are agreeing to at each point.

        1. 01

          Pick EMI as the payment method

          It sits alongside UPI, card and net banking in the payment step. Choosing it does not charge anything yet.

        2. 02

          Choose the bank and the tenure

          Only the banks that accepted your eligibility check are listed. The monthly figure shown there is the one from this page, to the rupee.

        3. 03

          Let the bank verify you

          You are handed to your bank's own secure page. ElectroMart never sees or stores your bank credentials, and the approval decision is the bank's alone.

        4. 04

          Confirm the mandate

          The bank sets up the auto-deduction. Your first collection is one month after the plan starts, and your first EMI is usually collected along with it or at the next collection date.

        What the monthly figure does not include

        • The processing fee, and the tax charged on that fee. A zero-cost plan still has both.
        • Late payment charges if a collection bounces. A returned mandate is also often treated as a missed payment on your record.
        • Prepayment charges, if your plan carries them. Ask the bank before you pay it off early, and check whether a part-prepayment is treated as a full one.
        • The cost of returning the goods. Cancelling an instalment plan does not cancel the order, and the two are settled separately.

        Read the sanction letter before you sign. It is the only document that carries your actual rate, your actual fee and your actual obligations.
