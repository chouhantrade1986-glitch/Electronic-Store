Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ElectroMart E-Gift Cards.

How does the recipient receive their ElectroMart Gift Card?
After confirmed online payment, your claim code appears in your ElectroMart account under gift cards. Codes use the format EM-GC-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-XXXXXX.
What is the validity of ElectroMart Gift Cards?
The remaining balance and redemption status are shown in your ElectroMart account.
Can I use multiple gift cards on a single purchase?
Yes! When you redeem your gift cards, the full amount is added to your ElectroMart Pay wallet balance. You can stack as many gift cards as you want and combine them with UPI, debit/credit cards or net banking at checkout.
