How the arithmetic works

  • Earn 10 points for every ₹100 you spend on ElectroMart.
  • Redeem at a flat rate: 100 points are worth exactly ₹1. The rate is the same in every tier, so a Platinum member and a Bronze member convert points identically.
  • Tiers unlock at 5,000, 20,000 and 50,000 points and add shipping, early access and support benefits on top of the same conversion rate.
  • You can redeem up to half the value of an order, capped at 50,000 points per order.

Points do not expire while your membership is active. Reward availability and partner terms are set by each partner and shown on each offer.
