1. How can I track my order?

Go to the Orders page and search by order ID or product name to view status updates, or use our 24x7 Help Center.

Accepted methods depend on your profile settings and checkout availability for your region, including ElectroMart Pay, UPI, Cards, and NetBanking.

3. Can I cancel an order?

Orders can usually be canceled before shipment. Once shipped, use the return and refund policy flow.

4. How long does shipping take?

Delivery times vary by location and method. See the Shipping Policy page for full details.

5. How do refunds work?

Refund eligibility and timelines are explained on the Refund Policy page and in the Returns Center.

Use our 24x7 Customer Service & Help Center (help.html) to chat with our assistant, request an instant callback, or submit an inquiry ticket.
